      Step 1

      How to use

      1. Do one Reading practice test. Cambridge IELTS books are best.
      2. Check your answers. Keep the reading text open next to you.
      3. For each wrong answer, tap Check a wrong answer. Pick the kind of question, type your answer and the right answer. The app may ask you 1–3 easy yes/no questions.
      4. Type your score out of 40 under My scores.
      5. Look at Your top 3 mistakes. Before the next test, practise only the fix for #1.

      Tip: add this page to your home screen so it opens like an app. iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen. Android: ⋮ → Add to Home screen.

      The tips are based on official IELTS guidance from IDP and the British Council. Band tables: IDP IELTS. Not affiliated with IELTS, IDP, the British Council or Cambridge.
